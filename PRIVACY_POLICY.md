# Privacy Policy for Raw Fabric Automation

**Last updated: September 2026**

Raw Fabric Automation ("we", "our") provides automated Facebook messaging and order processing services for our Facebook Page **Raw Fabric**.

### 1. Information We Collect
We only collect information voluntarily provided by customers via Facebook Messenger or comments, specifically:
- Customer Name
- Delivery Address
- Phone Number
This information is solely collected for order fulfillment and delivery purposes.

### 2. How We Use Information
The collected information is used strictly to process Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, coordinate parcel delivery, and communicate order status to customers.

### 3. Data Protection and Security
We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. All data is securely processed and stored for business management.
